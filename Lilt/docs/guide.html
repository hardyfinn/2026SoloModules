Lilt

Relabi generator · four LFOs that lean on the pulse — VCV Rack, 36 HP, plugin 2.0.0

What relabi is

John Berndt's word for rhythm that keeps almost having a steady beat. Instead of counting steps, you add up a few slow waves running at near-musical ratios of a pulse. Wherever the combined wave passes through a line, a hit happens. The hits cluster around the beat, slide off it, and find it again, because the waves line up and fall apart in long cycles. Lilt does exactly this, with four LFOs and four lines, and gives you knobs to decide how loose or locked it feels.

Quick start

Patch TRIG 1-4 into four drum voices (hat, rim, tom, something low) and BEAT into a kick. The defaults run at 120 BPM with ratios 1, 3/4, 3/2 and 1/3 and a little DRIFT, so you'll hear a pattern that sits on the kick but never quite repeats. Turn GRID up to pull it into a 16th grid; turn DRIFT and MUTATE up to let it wander. Watch the screen: the cream line is the sum, the coloured lines are the four thresholds, and each dot is a trigger.

Pulse and clock

PULSEThe felt beat, 20 to 300 BPM. The yellow light flashes on each pulse. PULSE CV is 1 V/oct (+1 V doubles the tempo).
CLOCKLocks the pulse to an incoming clock (PULSE knob is then ignored). Right-click picks 1, 2, 4 (default, 16ths) or 24 clocks per beat. With a clock patched, PULSE CV speeds the LFOs up or down against the clock instead.
RESETEvery LFO jumps back to the start of its cycle and the pulse to the downbeat: "back to the one". A trigger every 4 or 8 bars gives the wandering a phrase shape.
BEAT outA trigger on every pulse, for the kick or for clocking other modules.

The four LFOs

RATIOCycles per pulse, 1/8 to 8. With SNAP on it lands on 1/8, 1/6, 1/4, 1/3, 3/8, 1/2, 2/3, 3/4, 1, 5/4, 4/3, 3/2, 2, 5/2, 3, 4, 6 or 8. With SNAP off it is continuous (odd values give very long cycles). The screen's right side shows the real ratios, including drift.
LEVELHow much that LFO counts in the sum. Left of centre flips it upside down; centre takes it out. The sum is scaled so it always fills the screen.
OUTThe raw LFO, ±5 V, for modulating anything else in time with the rhythm.
SHAPEAll four: sine, triangle (middle), soft square (right). Squarer shapes make the hits snap into tighter clusters.

Steering: loose or locked

DRIFTPulls each LFO off its ratio by its own amount, up to 15%. At 0 the pattern repeats exactly (every 12 pulses with the defaults). A little drift makes it slowly evolve; a lot makes it float. Right-click "Re-roll drift directions" picks new amounts.
MUTATEKeeps changing the drift amounts, nudges the LFO phases and wobbles their levels, so the pattern never settles. At 0 it is still.
FREEZEWhile this gate is high MUTATE stops where it is (red light). Use it to hold a moment you like, then let go.
GRIDPulls triggers forward onto the next 1/4-pulse tick. 0 is pure relabi; half-way, hits close to a tick snap to it and the rest stay loose; full is fully quantised (the hits still come from the waves, so it's still not a loop).
GRID plus MUTATE is the arrangement control: a track can open locked (GRID full, MUTATE 0), loosen in the break (GRID down, MUTATE up) and snap back for the drop. Automate both from Live or a slow LFO.

Thresholds and outputs

THRESHWhere the four lines sit (−5 to +5 V on the SUM scale). Low = more hits, high = fewer.
SPREADHow far apart the lines are. Line 1 is lowest (busiest), line 4 highest (rarest, good for accents). At 0 all four fire together.
EDGEUP: fire when the sum climbs through a line. DOWN: when it falls through. BOTH: either way, and the GATE toggles on each hit (on, off, on…), which gives on/off sections.
TRIG 1-41 ms triggers; the small light flashes.
GATE 1-4High from the hit until the sum leaves that side of the line, so gate length follows the waves: long when they move slowly, short when fast.
SUMThe combined wave, ±5 V. A great modulation source in its own right.
ACC0-10 V: how steeply the sum crossed at the last hit. Fast crossings are loud, slow ones soft. Use it for velocity or decay.
CV rowPULSE, DRIFT, MUTATE, THRESH, SPREAD, GRID, each with an attenuverter (full right = 10 V covers the knob's range; THRESH ±5 V).

Patch ideas

Detroit leanLive clock (CV Tools through the disting NT) into CLOCK, 4 per beat. TRIG 1 to hats, TRIG 3 to a rim, ACC into hat decay. GRID 40%, DRIFT 25%. The hats push and pull against a straight kick.
Autechre driftSNAP off, ratios near 0.9, 1.1, 1.6, 0.37. DRIFT 60%, MUTATE 40%, EDGE BOTH. GATE 2 and 4 open and close a VCA on a pad; TRIG 1 to a Boohm kick. Pulse a slow LFO into FREEZE so it alternates between evolving and holding.
Phrase resetBEAT into a divider (Pam's or a DROID clock divider) set to /32, into RESET. Every 8 bars the pattern returns to the one, then wanders off again.
Melodic liltSUM into a quantiser, TRIG 2 clocks a sample and hold on it, into Just Friends or Mangrove pitch. Notes and rhythm come from the same waves, so they agree.
Modulation bankIgnore the triggers: the four OUTs are four related LFOs that stay in a slowly shifting relationship to the tempo. Send them to filter cutoffs in a mix.
