Cymbolism Extended

The same cymbal, with the laws of physics on knobs · VCV Rack · Hær · 2.4.0 · 60 HP · stereo
New in 2.4.0: Cymbolism Lite, the same cymbal with size, weight, rivets and beaters held inside a range that stays light however it is set or randomized. It has its own manual.

What it is

Cymbolism Extended is the Cymbolism engine with its physics opened up. Everything Cymbolism does is still here and sounds the same: with the UNREAL row at its neutral settings, the output matches Cymbolism sample for sample. Turn the UNREAL knobs and the cymbal starts breaking rules no real metal can: its pitches can be bent, mirrored or tuned to a scale, it can sing on its own forever, bloom in slow motion, or morph into another instrument while it rings.

It also adds six new types, three new materials, a hole, wider shape ranges, stereo mics, a contact pickup, a bouncing beater, rain, and extra outputs for the low core, the high wash and an envelope.

The UNREAL row (green ground) is where the strange half lives. Its neutral settings are: MORPH 0, WARP +1 (centre detent), TUNE 0, TILT 0, SUSTAIN 0, FLOW centre, LOAD 0. "Reset the UNREAL section" in the context menu puts them all back.

The new types and materials

TypeSizeEdgeCharacter
GONG24"1.6 mmtam-tam style, rim turned back: the slowest, biggest bloom. Try a MALLET and a hard hit.
SHEET20"0.8 mmthunder sheet: flat, no bell, extremely nonlinear.
BOWL8"3 mmsinging bowl, a deep bowl shape. Made for BOW.

RING14"2 mmring gong: a flat disc with a hole 60% of the radius.
DOME10"1.5 mma cymbal that is all bell.

GONG and SHEET are smaller than the real things (a tam-tam would be 28" or more, a thunder sheet 30" and 0.3 mm) so that they build in about two seconds instead of thirty. Turn SIZE up for bigger ones: above 1.5x the type's size the model is rebuilt with a wider band so the top octave is not empty.

ALLOY adds ALUMINIUM (lighter and brighter, moderate loss), GLASS (very low loss, nearly flat with frequency: rings for ages) and WOOD (loss rising with the square of frequency: the highs die in milliseconds, leaving a knock).

Row 2: UNREAL

MORPHSlides every mode's pitch from this cymbal's towards TYPE B's, on a log scale, while it rings. The body, the bloom and the mic stay the main cymbal's, so at 100% it is B's pitches on A's body. 0 is neutral.
WARPBends the frequency law around PIVOT. +1 is real (detent). Above 1 the spectrum stretches: wide, sparse, glassy. Between 0 and 1 it compresses: dense, gong-like. At 0 every mode sits on the pivot: one huge beating unison. Below 0 the spectrum is mirrored: the big slow shapes ring high and the fine ripples ring low.
PIVOTThe pitch WARP bends around, 50 Hz to 5 kHz.
TUNEPulls every mode towards the nearest note of SCALE. At 100% a few cents of random spread is kept so the shimmer survives, and paired modes still beat.
SCALECHROMATIC, MAJOR, MINOR, DORIAN, PENT MAJ, PENT MIN, BLUES, WHOLE TONE, FIFTHS, OCTAVES, HARMONIC (the harmonic series of the root), CHORD (the notes on the CHORD input; unpatched, a minor ninth).

TILTTilts how decay depends on pitch. Negative: the highs outlast the lows, which no real plate does. Positive: an exaggerated, quickly darkening ring.

SWELLHow fast SUSTAIN grows the ring, 0.5 to 30 per second: the length of a reverse cymbal.
COLOURWhere SUSTAIN feeds energy in, 60 Hz to 10 kHz. BLOOM then spreads it around.
FLOWThe speed of the bloom, 0.05x to 10x (centre detent is real). Slow: a crash that takes seconds to climb. Fast: the wash is there at once and burns out.
LOADA weight stuck to the cymbal, 0 to 500 g. Only modes that move where it sits drop in pitch, so pairs split and beat.

Jacks

STRIKE, VEL, CHOKE, ROLL and EXCITE as Cymbolism. HOLD (gate: SUSTAIN while high). FREEZE (gate, adds to the button). PUMP (audio: modulates the tension at audio rate, see below). V/OCT (transposes every mode). CHORD (polyphonic V/oct, up to 16 notes, any octave: the notes for SCALE CHORD). Every knob except FLANGE, HOLE, ALLOY and SCALE has a CV jack under it (CV adds to the knob; 10 V covers its full range).

Outputs: L and R (stereo; L is mono when R is unpatched), CORE (the coupled low modes only), WASH (everything above them), ENV (0 to 10 V, follows the energy of the core: use the cymbal as a modulation source).

PUMP is parametric excitation. An oscillator at twice a mode's frequency makes that mode grow from nothing once the pump is deep enough; the threshold depends on the mode, so start around 5 V and adjust. Below the threshold it just warbles the pitch. Feeding it an oscillator, or the module's own output through a VCA, gives subharmonic tones no strike can.

Screen and context menu

The screen is Cymbolism's, plus a line naming the morph target, scale and root, and a thin strip that draws the current frequency law (a straight diagonal when it is real).

The menu is Cymbolism's (quality ECO, MID, HIGH or ULTRA, own thread, keep geometry, rebuild on CV, playing defaults, OUT trim), plus:

  • Reset the UNREAL section: every UNREAL knob back to neutral.
  • Tuning follows V/OCT only at strikes: V/OCT is read at each strike and held, like a sample-and-hold, instead of gliding the ringing cymbal.

Patch recipes

1. Tuned ride

  • Setup: RIDE, TUNE 100%, SCALE CHORD, a chord sequencer into CHORD (polyphonic).
  • Result: the ride's wash locks to each chord as it changes. TUNE 60 to 80% keeps more of the metal.

2. Reverse cymbal

  • Setup: SUSTAIN about 70%, a gate into HOLD, SWELL to taste (lower is longer), and a trigger into CHOKE on the downbeat.
  • Result: the cymbal swells up out of nothing while the gate is high and is cut dead on the beat.

3. Acid bell

  • Setup: BELL or PLATE, TUNE 100%, SCALE HARMONIC, V/OCT and STRIKE from a bassline sequencer, DECAY short.
  • Variation: tick "Tuning follows V/OCT only at strikes" for clean steps; leave it off and slew V/OCT for glides.

4. Endless crash

  • Setup: CRASH, SUSTAIN 60 to 100%, BLOOM 1.5, a slow LFO into COLOUR.
  • Result: a crash that never dies, its colour wandering as the bloom keeps moving energy around.

5. Melting cymbal

  • Setup: CRASH with TYPE B on CHINA or GONG. An envelope (a second or two) into MORPH, fired with each strike.
  • Result: each hit starts as one instrument and melts into the other.

6. Mirror metal

  • Setup: WARP fully down (-1), sweep PIVOT by hand.
  • Result: an inside-out spectrum: the bloom pours energy downwards instead of up.

7. Bowed bowl

  • Setup: BOWL with PLAY BOW, ROLL held high, FORCE for pressure, RATE for bow speed.
  • Variation: GLASS alloy for a glass harmonica, or SUSTAIN on to keep it singing after the bow lifts.

8. Slow-motion crash

  • Setup: GONG, FLOW at minimum, a hard hit with a mallet.
  • Result: the bloom takes seconds to climb: a crash filmed at high speed.

9. Rain on a sheet

  • Setup: SHEET with PLAY RAIN and ROLL held high. RATE for density, FORCE for drop size.
  • Variation: BOUNCE up and PLAY TIP instead, for hail.

10. Dub siren plate

  • Setup: PLATE, SUSTAIN on, a slow triangle LFO into V/OCT, one strike.
  • Result: a singing plate sweeping up and down like a siren. Add a delay.

11. Pumped subharmonics

  • Setup: PLATE or BOWL, a sine oscillator into PUMP at 5 V, tuned to about twice a low mode (sweep it slowly until something grows).
  • Result: a mode wakes up on its own at half the oscillator's pitch, with no strike.

Notes and limits

  • The UNREAL settings can make the cymbal much louder or quieter than a real one. A fixed make-up gain follows WARP, but it is not exact: a crash at WARP -1 comes out about 14 dB quieter than at +1, and a ride with every mode near one pitch (WARP 0) about 11 dB louder. A soft limiter above 8 V keeps the outputs within 10 V.
  • Under extreme settings (BLOOM above about 1, hardest hits, strongly bent laws) the engine's energy guard may step in for a moment to keep the cymbal stable. You hear a brief dip, never a blow-up.
  • CPU is as Cymbolism: about 19% (ECO), 27% (MID), 37% (HIGH) and 85% (ULTRA) of a cloud machine's core for a loud crash, less on an Apple silicon Mac. A silent cymbal costs nothing, but SUSTAIN, FREEZE and PUMP keep it awake.
  • MORPH, WARP, TUNE, TILT, LOAD and TENSION retune the modes live on the engine thread; they cost a little CPU while they move and nothing when they stand still.
  • Very large SIZE knob settings and SHEET or GONG at big sizes have many thousands of modes, so they rebuild in a few seconds and cost more CPU. Use ECO quality if needed.
  • Sixteen types and nine alloys exist only in Extended. Cymbolism itself is unchanged.
