## Answer 2
Rough estimate: the next ae_live batch is more likely to land in 2027 than in the next few weeks. A drop before the end of 2026 is possible but unlikely.

**What I found**
- The latest batch I could confirm is 12 live recordings (the headline says 12, the list has 19), released around [5 Nov 2024](https://www.clashmusic.com/news/autechre-just-released-12-new-live-recordings/). It covers shows from 2022 to May 2024. That is about 6 months after the newest show in it, and about 15 months after the Aug 2023 AE_2022– release.
- The 2026 European tour was 24 shows, 24 Sep to 24 Oct, per [RA](https://ra.co/news/84125). I couldn't confirm the exact last show.
- [Past gaps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AE_LIVE) between a tour and its release vary a lot:
  - 2014–15 tour: recordings appeared within days or weeks of the shows, so a very fast drop does happen.
  - 2016/2018 shows: released April 2020, roughly 1.5 years after the last show.
  - 2022–2024 shows: released in batches, 6 to 15 months after the shows.

**Estimate:** the newer pattern is a lag of about 6 to 12 months after the shows, with one batch roughly every year. That puts the 2026 tour recordings in roughly spring to autumn 2027. They may be bundled with 2025 shows.

I couldn't check whether a 2025 batch has already come out. I also couldn't see the Bandcamp or store release history, so a quick look at the Autechre store would confirm the date of the last batch.
