# Lancing to Worthing distance

## Summary
About 3 miles (4.5 to 5 km) on foot, roughly an hour.
By train it's 5 to 6 minutes on Southern, stopping at East Worthing.

## Question
> How far from lancing station to worthing station

## Answer
It's about 3 miles (roughly 4.5 to 5 km) on foot, which takes about an hour at a normal pace. The two stations are about 2.4 miles apart as the crow flies, and the walk mostly follows the roads through East Worthing.

By train it's around 5 to 6 minutes on Southern, with one stop in between at East Worthing.

I worked these figures out from where the stations are rather than from a route planner, since the search results didn't give exact numbers, so treat them as close estimates.
