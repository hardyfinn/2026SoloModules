# Stem separation state of play

## Summary
Stem separation has kept improving, mainly in more stem types (guitar, piano, individual drum pieces) and in being built into DAWs.
Logic Pro topped a MusicRadar test of 11 tools; free UVR is among the best for vocals; artifacts remain on dense or old mono material.

## Question
> Here's a question about stem separation of audio files. I was very impressed by how good the stem separation technology had got about 1 year ago. Have there been improvements in this area still? What is possible to do today?

## Answer
Yes, it has kept improving, mainly in breadth (more instruments) and convenience (built into DAWs), though artifacts remain on hard material.

**What's better than a year ago**
- **More stems:** The old vocals/drums/bass/other split is now often six or more. Logic Pro 11.2 splits out guitar and piano. LALAL.AI offers about ten types (electric and acoustic guitar, piano, synth, strings). SpectraLayers 12 can also split sax/brass and lead from backing vocals.
- **Drum pieces:** SpectraLayers, RipX, LALAL.AI Pro and UVR ensembles can isolate kick, snare and hi-hat. Logic and RX can't.
- **Better models:** BS-Roformer models reportedly took over the open-source leaderboards in 2025–26, and free tools are now close to paid cloud services.
- **In your DAW:** Logic, Cubase 15, Ableton, FL Studio and Studio One all have built-in separation now.

**How they compare:** In a [MusicRadar test of 11 tools](https://www.musicradar.com/music-tech/i-tested-11-of-the-best-stem-separation-tools-and-you-might-already-have-the-winner-in-your-daw), Logic Pro scored best (16/20), ahead of SpectraLayers and LALAL.AI (both 15). Free UVR was among the best for vocal-only extraction. A [tool roundup](https://mixinggpt.com/blog/best-ai-stem-separation-tools-2026) also covers RipX and Moises.

**Still limited:** Every tool left some warbling artifacts, especially on dense rock, old mono recordings and similar-sounding parts. Cloud services need you to upload your audio.

For an old record like Hot Lemonade, UVR or SpectraLayers would be my starting point. I can set up a local workflow for you if you want to try it.
