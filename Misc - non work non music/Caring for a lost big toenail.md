# Caring for a lost big toenail

## Summary
Keep the exposed nail bed clean, dry and covered, wear roomy shoes, and expect 12 to 18 months for full regrowth.
See a GP or pharmacist for signs of infection or if you have diabetes or poor circulation.

## Question
> I've just lost my big toe nails, after 2 weeks of it hanging on. How should I care for my toe?

## Answer
Sorry, that's sore. General care for the exposed nail bed:

- **Clean:** wash gently once or twice a day with mild soap and water, pat dry, and don't pick at any loose edges.
- **Protect:** put on a non-stick dressing or plaster, and change it daily or when it gets wet. A dab of petroleum jelly under it keeps it from sticking.
- **Footwear:** wear roomy shoes or open sandals for a few weeks, and avoid sports or running until it's comfortable.
- **Keep it dry:** avoid long soaks and swimming until the skin has toughened.
- **Regrowth:** a toenail takes roughly 12 to 18 months to grow back fully. It may come in thick or ridged at first, which is normal.

See a GP or pharmacist if you notice spreading redness, warmth, swelling, pus, a bad smell, increasing pain, or fever. Also check in if the nail bed bleeds heavily, or if you have diabetes, poor circulation or a weakened immune system, since foot wounds need closer watching. If the nails keep coming off without an injury, mention it too, because fungal infection or a running or footwear issue could be behind it.
