# TR-808 and TR-909 samples

## Summary
The TR-808 is fully analog with no samples.
The TR-909 uses 6-bit ROM samples only for its cymbals and hats; the rest is analog.

## Question
> New thread - which instruments on rolands tr808 and tr909 are samples?

## Answer
Only the TR-909 uses samples, and only for its cymbals. The TR-808 has none.

- **TR-808:** every sound is analog, including the hi-hats, cymbal and cowbell. The hats and cymbal come from six square-wave oscillators run through filters.
- **TR-909:** the open hat, closed hat, crash and ride are 6-bit samples stored in ROM. The kick, snare, toms, rimshot and clap are analog.
