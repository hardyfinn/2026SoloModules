# Hove to Brighton station walk

## Summary
About 1.5 miles (2.5 km), 30 to 35 minutes on foot via Cromwell Road and Seven Dials.
The seafront route adds about half a mile.

## Question
> How far is the walk from hove station to brighton station?

## Answer
It's about 1.5 miles (roughly 2.5 km), which takes most people 30 to 35 minutes at a normal pace.

The simplest route: leave Hove station on Goldstone Villas, turn left onto Cromwell Road and follow it east (it runs into Davigdor Road) to Seven Dials. From there take Buckingham Place or Terminus Road down to Brighton station. If you want the seafront instead, go south to Church Road and follow Western Road east, then up Queens Road. That adds about half a mile.

I couldn't find a map source that confirms the exact figure ([Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Hove/Brighton) lists walking as an option), so this is from my own knowledge of the area. Google Maps will give you the exact number.
