# Cocktail bars near Baqueano Brighton

## Summary
The closest good cocktail bars to Baqueano (125 Western Road) are Apiary and Bar Medusa, both about 5 minutes' walk.
The Grand/Cyan, which won Best Cocktails at BRAVO 2026, is about 10 minutes away.

## Answer
Baqueano is at 125 Western Road, right where Brighton turns into Hove. Here are the good cocktail spots closest to it, roughly nearest first:

- **Apiary** (Norfolk Square, about 5 minutes' walk west). A small bar built around honey and mead cocktails, and it's in the 2026 BRAVO top 20.
- **Bar Medusa** (Preston Street, about 5 minutes south). A small, fairy-lit cocktail bar, good for a quiet drink after dinner.
- **The Grand / Cyan** (Kings Road seafront, about 10 minutes). Won Best Cocktails in Brighton at the 2026 BRAVO awards, so this is the one to pick if you want something a bit special.
- **Clementine and Co** (Duke Street, about 10 minutes east). Came third in BRAVO 2026, with premium spirits and good low and no-alcohol options.
- **Burnt Orange** and **Twisted Lemon** (both off Middle Street, about 10 to 12 minutes). Burnt Orange is the smarter of the two. Twisted Lemon is a hidden spot down an alley with a huge menu organised by spirit.
- **The Golden Pineapple** (Ship Street, about 12 minutes). Tequila and mezcal specialists.

The walking times are my estimates from the map, not measured. Baqueano is open 5 to 10pm on Sundays, but I haven't checked Sunday hours for the bars. Are you going tonight, and is it a date or a group? Tell me and I'll narrow it down to one or two and check they're open.
