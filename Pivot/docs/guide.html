Pivot

Metric modulation clock. VCV Rack, 30 HP, plugin 2.0.0, MIT licence

What it does

Metric modulation changes tempo by re-hearing a note value as the new beat. If the dotted 8th of the old tempo becomes the quarter note of the new one, 120 BPM turns into 160, and nothing breaks: the first beat of the new tempo lands exactly where a dotted 8th of the old tempo would have landed. Pivot follows your clock from Live and plays out a chain of these changes. It can snap or glide between them, keep a ghost of the tempo you just left, and land back home exactly on Live's bar line, so your arrangement in Live never drifts.

Each step reads OLD = NEW: the OLD note value, at the old tempo, lasts as long as the NEW note value at the new tempo. New tempo = old tempo × NEW ÷ OLD. The screen shows every step's ratio and the tempo it gives, and steps multiply along the chain.

Quick start (Live as master clock)

1. Get Live's clock into Rack. Either use VCV MIDI to CV receiving Live's MIDI clock (CLK output at 24 PPQN, START or RESET into Pivot's RESET), or send a clock from CV Tools through the disting NT. Set Pivot's IN knob to match: 24 for MIDI clock, 4 if the clock ticks in 16ths.
2. Patch Pivot's CLOCK out into your sequencers (set OUT to the resolution they want: 4 PPQN = 16ths) and RESET out into their reset inputs.
3. Press 1 on the chain. At the next bar line, the tempo pivots from 120 to 160. Press HOME, and Pivot eases back over two bars to land exactly on one of Live's downbeats.
With nothing in CLOCK, Pivot runs from its own BPM knob.

Controls

HOME, 1-8The chain. HOME is Live's tempo. Press a step to go there on the next QUANT point. The lit step is current, a blinking one is queued, and dim ones are inside LENGTH.
OLD / NEWThe two note values of each step: 1/16T, 1/16Q (quintuplet), 1/16, 1/8T, 1/16., 1/8Q, 1/8, 1/4T, 1/8., 1/4, 1/2T, 1/4., 1/2. The screen shows the step's ratio and the tempo you end up at (relative to home).
BPMHome tempo when CLOCK is unpatched.
BARBeats per bar (2 to 12). Sets where bar lines fall for QUANT, BAR out, AUTO and LAND.
IN / OUTClock resolution in and out: 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 24 or 48 pulses per quarter note.
LENGTHHow many steps NEXT and AUTO walk through.
AUTOOff, or move on every 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 or 32 bars. This gives you an arrangement that plays itself.
GLIDESnap, or slide into the new tempo over 1 beat, 2 beats, or 1 to 16 bars (accelerando and ritardando). The phase never breaks.
NEXTGo to the next step. Its light shows while a glide or landing is under way.
QUANTWhen a change happens: on the next BAR, on the next BEAT, or NOW. Beats and bars here are Pivot's own (the pivoted grid).
AT ENDAfter the last step, NEXT goes HOME, LOOPs to step 1 (itself a metric modulation), or HOLDs.
LANDON: going home bends the tempo slightly over at least 2 bars, so Pivot's downbeat arrives exactly on Live's downbeat. OFF: home tempo straight away (or by GLIDE). The phase stays wherever it was, which is great for a deliberate offset.

Inputs and outputs

CLOCK inFrom Live (MIDI to CV, CV Tools, the disting NT). Pivot measures the tempo, so Live's tempo changes are followed too.
RESET inBack to bar 1 and home. Send it with Live's start so bar lines match.
NEXT, HOME inTriggers, same as the buttons. Try clips in Live sending these at section changes.
SELECT in1 V per step: 0 V = home, 1 V = step 1 ... 8 V = step 8. A step sequencer or Marbles' quantised X can choose tempos.
CLOCK outThe pivoted clock at OUT resolution (50% gate).
BEAT, BARA trigger on every pivoted beat and bar.
RESET outPasses resets on, for downstream sequencers.
PIVOTA trigger whenever a change starts. Fire a crash, a filter sweep or a Marbles re-seed.
GHOSTThe clock of the tempo you just left, still running in phase. Patch it into hats or a second sequencer for a few bars of two tempos at once.
RATIOThe tempo ratio as 1 V per doubling (0 V = home, +0.415 V = ×4/3). Use it as V/oct to transpose a voice with the tempo, or to open filters as the music speeds up.
AWAYHigh whenever you are not home (including while landing). Use it to mute, unmute or crossfade parts.

Recipes

1. Detroit lift. Chain: 1/8.=1/4 (×4/3), then 1/4=1/8. (×3/4, back to home tempo). AUTO 8 bars, LENGTH 2, AT END HOME, LAND ON. Every 8 bars the groove jumps up a gear and drops back, and it re-locks to Live every cycle. Patch GHOST into a closed hat so the old tempo carries on under the new one.

2. Autechre drift. GLIDE 4 bars, QUANT BEAT, LAND OFF. Drive SELECT with Marbles X (quantised, small range) or a slow random S&H so Pivot wanders between ×5/4, ×4/5 and ×3/2 without ever repeating a path. Send RATIO to a filter cutoff so the timbre tracks the speed.

3. Triplet floor. One step: 1/8T=1/8 (×3/2). Kick and bass on Pivot's BEAT, hats on Live's clock direct. Hit NEXT for a section: the kick is now on the old triplets, while the hats stay where they were.

4. Live drives the arrangement. Use CV Tools or MIDI notes from clips to fire NEXT and HOME at section boundaries. AWAY turns on a breakdown layer while you are off home.

Tip: keep a sequencer on Live's clock as an anchor (a kick or a pad) and let the others run on Pivot. The tension between the two grids is the point, and LAND resolves it.

Demo

pivot-demo.wav, 96 s: AUTO every 4 bars through steps 1 to 4 and home (×4/3, ×3/4, ×3/2, ×2/3), snap in the first half and 1-bar glides in the second. The kick, snare and hats follow Pivot, a rim plays the GHOST clock, and a low bell marks Live's own bar lines, so you can hear Pivot leave the grid and land back on it.

Notes

Pivot follows the clock pulse by pulse and fills the gaps from the measured period, so the pivoted clock stays smooth even from a 1 PPQN source. If the clock stops, Pivot stops with it. Tempo ratios are capped at ×1/16 to ×16 of home. Untested in Rack so far: please tell me if anything is off.
