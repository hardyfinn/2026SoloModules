Heathrow

Stereo phaser · flanger · through-zero · second engine · 6 memories — VCV Rack, 42 HP, plugin 2.0.0

Quick start

Patch audio into IN L (mono is fine, IN R is normalled), take OUT L and OUT R. The defaults give an 8-stage phaser sweeping slowly. Turn MORPH right for a flanger, FEEDBACK either way for character, and MIX past half-way for vibrato.

Engine A

MORPHPhaser (left) to flanger (right). The middle plays both at once: phaser notches plus a comb.
CENTERWhere the sweep sits: phaser 40 Hz to 16 kHz, flanger 15 ms to 0.15 ms.
DEPTHHow far the LFO moves the sweep around CENTER.
RATE0.01 to 20 Hz. RATE CV is 1 V/oct and goes up to 2 kHz for audio-rate FM. In CLK mode it picks cycles per clock: 1/64, 1/32, 1/16, 1/12, 1/8, 1/6, 1/4, 1/3, 1/2, 1, 2x, 3x, 4x.
FEEDBACKNegative: hollow, nasal. Positive: ringing, metallic. Safe up to the ends (soft-clipped).
STAGES2 to 24 phaser stages; every 2 stages add a notch.
RESSharper, more resonant phaser notches.
SPREADOffsets the right channel's LFO, 0 to 180°. 90° is the classic wide stereo swirl.
SHAPEsine → triangle → ramp → square → drift (smooth random) → S&H, blending in between.
STEPSOFF, or 2 to 16 jumps per LFO cycle. With CLK this gives rhythmic sweeps locked to the groove. GLIDE rounds the jumps off.
MIXCentre: dry. Half-way either side: 50/50, the deepest notches. Ends: wet only (vibrato). The left side flips the wet's polarity, which moves the notches.
ENVThe input's level pushes the sweep up (right) or down (left): an auto-wah-like phaser.
TZThrough-zero: the dry is delayed 3 ms, and the flanger delay sweeps 0 to 6 ms across it. CENTER at noon is the zero point. Use MIX on the left side for the deepest jet. Phaser and engine B also hear the delayed dry, so everything stays in time.
LFOFREE runs on RATE. CLK locks to CLOCK; RESET restarts both LFOs.
SWEEPReplaces A's LFO (±5 V = full depth). Plug Marbles, an envelope or an audio oscillator. A 2-channel poly cable drives left and right separately.
LFO outA's LFO (±5 V) to sync other modules to the sweep.
CV rowMORPH, CENTER, DEPTH, RATE, FDBK, STAGES, SPREAD, MIX with attenuverters (full right = 10 V covers the knob's range; FDBK and MIX ±5 V).

Engine B

A second engine like the two phasers in a Mu-Tron Bi-Phase. B has its own MORPH, CENTER, DEPTH, RATE, FDBK, STAGES and MIX, each with a CV input (no attenuverter). It shares RES, TZ, SHAPE, STEPS, SPREAD, GLIDE, ENV and the clock with A.

B ON/OFFSwitches B out completely.
ROUTESERIES: A then B (two phasers in a row make a 48-stage monster). PARALLEL: A and B side by side, averaged. SPLIT: A on the left, B on the right, so one mono input becomes two different sweeps.
B SWEEPOWN: B's own LFO at B RATE (clock-locked in CLK mode). A: follows A's sweep. A INV: A's sweep upside down, so when A goes up, B comes down.

Memory

Tap SAVE (slots blink), then tap a slot to store. Tap a slot to recall it.
Slots 1-6Each holds every setting: all of A and B, the switches and attenuverters. Bright = the current slot, dim = has something stored.
TIMEHow long knobs take to glide to the recalled settings: 0 jumps, then 10 ms up to 20 s. Switches change straight away.
SLOT CV0-10 V picks slot 1-6 (about 1.67 V per slot). Whenever the chosen slot changes it is recalled, with TIME. A stepped sequencer here gives scene changes per bar.
Right-clickClear one slot, or all. Slots are saved in the patch.

Patch ideas

Detroit pad washPhaser, 8 stages, FEEDBACK −50%, RATE slow, SPREAD 90°. B on, SPLIT, B SWEEP A INV: left and right sweep in opposite directions.
Clocked chopCLK mode, RATE 1/4, SHAPE S&H, STEPS 8, GLIDE a touch. The notch jumps to a new place every step, in time.
Tape jetMORPH full right, TZ on, MIX −50%, DEPTH full, RATE 0.1 Hz, FEEDBACK +40%. Works on drums and full mixes.
Autechre mutationMarbles X into SWEEP, Marbles Y into B CENTER, B on in SERIES. Sequence SLOT from a slow step pattern with TIME at 2-4 s so the effect morphs between scenes.
Audio-rate FMRATE CV from an oscillator tracking your bassline, DEPTH low, phaser 4 stages: metallic sidebands that follow the notes.
