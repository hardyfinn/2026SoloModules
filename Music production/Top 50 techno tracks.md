# Top 50 techno tracks

## Summary
Ranked top 50 lists built by counting how often each track appears across published lists.
Covers all-time techno, Chicago house before 2010, a list that includes The Wipe, and UK techno.

## Question
> compile a list of the 50 top techno tracks of all time based on lists online.

## Answer
Here's the list: top-50-techno-tracks.md. I ranked tracks by how many of 13 published lists include them.

Model 500's No UFO's comes out on top (7 lists), followed by Jeff Mills' The Bells (6), then Good Life, Energy Flash and Knights of the Jaguar (5 each). Strings of Life and Spastik are close behind.

Only 24 tracks appear on more than one list, so I filled places 25 to 50 with single-list picks grouped by era and scene. The file links every source list and notes where the counts are shaky. Several lists lean towards Detroit, and I couldn't read the Guardian's list directly.
