# Sound design between Detroit techno and Autechre

## Summary
Sound design suggestions for music between Detroit techno and Autechre, from a single synth patch to a modular patch.
A follow-up maps the ideas onto a specific Eurorack rig.

## Question
> sound design may be as simple as a patch on a vst synth, or as complex as a modular patch, but i would like to hear your suggestions considering the style of music I'm after.

## Answer
For something between Detroit and Autechre, the key idea is to keep the harmony warm and steady while every hit changes slightly in timbre. Here are three directions:

1. **Detroit chord bed.** Play Hapax chords into a slow-attack, slightly detuned saw pad or a string-machine VST. Add chorus and slow LFO movement on the filter. Keep the voicings simple so the chord playground's extensions carry the emotion.
2. **Autechre-style percussion.** Use short FM or Plaits-style hits from the disting NT or VCV. Send Marbles' X outputs to FM depth, decay and pitch, so each hit sounds slightly different even when the rhythm repeats. Set déjà vu around noon so the variation loops, then lock it when you find something you like.
3. **Resample and mangle.** Record a few minutes of the modular jamming into Live, then chop it with Simpler slices, Granulator or Beat Repeat. That gives you a second generation of material that's more yours than the raw patch.

## Question 2
> as an aside, my modular includes - pam's new workout, k-accumulator, disting nt, ohmforce bohm, zadar, vult freak, system80 810, mannequins mangrove, mannequins three sisters, mannequins just friends, mannequins cold mac. Metropolix sequencer, atlantix, marbles, befaco random8, droid system inc 3xM4, 2xB32, 2xP2B8 and master18.

## Answer 2
That's a serious rig, and it changes some of my earlier suggestions. Three things stand out:

- **DROID is your logic brain.** It can be the rig's clock divider, probability engine and logic in one place, which covers the earlier logic idea. A good setup would be Live's clock and reset going into DROID, and DROID then feeding Pam's, the Metropolix and Marbles. Cold Mac is also good for hands-on logic and crossfading.
- **Your evolving tools are already there.** The Metropolix accumulators, Marbles and Random8 together could drive an entire Autechre-style patch, and DROID could decide when each one is allowed to change.
- **Mannequins voices for the Detroit side.** Just Friends in plume mode, or Mangrove into Three Sisters, will give you warm, playable chord and pad tones alongside the Hapax.
