# Simple mixing upgrades

## Summary
A few simple steps that improve a minimal mix of level balance, sidechain and a master compressor.
Aimed at someone who wants better results without making mixing a big job.

## Question
> good suggestions. Mixing is minimal, as i'm not too interested in this step. I would be keen to understand how to do this better but simply. Currently I balance levels, mayve some sidechain compression  and put the master out through a mastering compressor plug in.

## Answer
You're already doing the important parts. Here are five simple upgrades that do most of the remaining work:

1. **Leave headroom.** Record modular audio so it peaks around -12 dB, and keep the master peaking around -6 dB before your mastering compressor.
2. **Give the low end to one thing.** Put EQ Eight on everything except kick and bass, with a high-pass filter around 100 to 150 Hz. That clears up most mud on its own.
3. **Use two groups.** Put drums on one group and everything else on another, with gentle Glue Compressor on the drum group (about 2 dB of reduction).
4. **Share your space.** Use one reverb and one delay on return tracks instead of on each channel, and high-pass those returns too.
5. **Check against a reference.** Drop in a track you love, turn it down to match your mix's loudness, and A/B it. Switch to mono now and then to check the bass holds up.

Save all of this as your Live default template once and you'll never set it up again.
