Recipes

Four complete setups for The Changes, Weighted and Long Form, now with drums from the Francois Markovian plugin. Long Form runs the song, The Changes plays the chords, Weighted writes the melody, and Markovian, Interlock, Rewrite and Governor play a drum part that follows the form.

The first recipe comes as a ready-made Rack patch, house-f-minor.vcv. The user guide explains every control of The Changes, Weighted and Long Form; this document is about putting them to work.

The Francois Markovian plugin: Francois Markovian (34 HP), Interlock (16 HP), Rewrite (28 HP) and Governor (22 HP). Panel renders, not Rack screenshots.
For Weighted plugin 2.6.0, LongFormMixxa 2.1.0 and Francois Markovian 2.5.0. November 2026.

The whole system

The top row makes the music's shape and harmony. The middle row is the drummer. Long Form sits over both: it tells The Changes when to change and which loop to play, and tells the drums which parts play and how busy to be.

Adding the Francois Markovian modules

Start from the sample setup in the user guide (Long Form, The Changes, Weighted, three synth voices and Mixxa). These six steps add the drum brain. You can stop after step 3 and add Rewrite and Governor later.

1
Clock. The same 16th clock that drives Long Form → CLOCK on Markovian, Interlock, Rewrite and Governor. If your clock has a reset, send it to every RESET too, so all five start on bar 1 together.
2
The chain. Markovian TRIG (poly) → Interlock TRIG IN. Interlock TRIG → Rewrite TRIG, and Interlock ACT → Rewrite ACT. Rewrite TRIG → Governor TRIG, and Rewrite ACT → Governor ACT.
3
Long Form arranges the drums. Long Form ACT → Interlock ACT IN. Long Form's lanes 1 to 6 now mute tracks A to F, and Rewrite and Governor hear the same mutes. Markovian's own letter mutes still work on top, for playing live.
4
Drum outs. Rewrite's mono outs A → kick, C → hats, D → clap, E → perc, each into Mixxa channels 1, 3, 4 and 5. Rewrite B → the bass envelope's gate: the bass now plays the drummer's rhythm, on The Changes' notes.
5
Energy. Long Form ENERGY → Governor's density TARGET CV, with the density TARGET knob fully left. Governor density CTRL → Markovian EVOLVE CV, with the EVOLVE knob fully left. Make snapshot Y the busier one.
6
Melody timing. Markovian DIV (set to /2) → Weighted TRIG and your random source's trigger. Long Form BAR stays on Weighted's ACCENT.

Settings that matter

ModuleSetting
Francois MarkovianDIV /2, OFFSET 0. The 8 steps are 8 sixteenths, which is half a bar, so step 1 and step 5 are the beats. Keep X close to straight and draw your fills into Y.
InterlockAMOUNT about 70%, MEMORY 1. Start with a handful of cells (below), not a full grid.
RewriteLOCK on A (kick) and D (clap) so the floor stays put. Leave the other tracks growing at the default rules. Rewrite passes triggers straight through until you press SEED.
GovernorWINDOW 32, density TARGET fully left (Long Form sets it), variety TARGET 30%, RESPONSE default, WOBBLE 0.

Optional extra links

  • Governor variety ERR → The Changes DRIFT CV: when the drums have been repeating, the chords drift more.
  • Long Form FILL → Markovian EVOLVE CV: snapshot Y plays at the end of every section.
  • The Changes TENSION → Interlock AMOUNT CV: the parts answer each other more on tense chords.
  • Governor SAME → Rewrite REWRITE NOW, VARIED → HOLD: the drums grow only when they've gone stale.

Recipe 1House in F minor: the ready-made patch

The patch house-f-minor.vcv holds the whole system: the user guide's sample setup plus the drum brain, with simple drum and synth voices from VCV Fundamental. Open it with File → Open in Rack. You need the Weighted, LongFormMixxa and Francois Markovian plugins installed, plus VCV Fundamental, which comes with Rack.

Before you press anything: click the Audio module (bottom row, right of Mixxa) and choose your audio device. Rack starts running straight away, so the track begins as soon as sound can get out.

What's in the patch

RowModules
1LFO (the 124 BPM 16th clock), Long Form, The Changes, Weighted, Random
2Francois Markovian, Interlock, Rewrite, Governor
3Drum voices: kick (VCO, ADSR, VCA), hats and clap (one Noise into two filters), perc
4Bass, pad and lead voices (VCO, VCF, ADSR, VCA each), Delay on the lead
5Mixxa (kick ducks the bass, pad and lead; GLUE 30%) and Audio

Cables are colour-coded: red clock, yellow LINK, purple HARM, green pitch, blue gates, orange triggers, light green ACT, violet CVs, white audio.

The beat (Markovian, snapshot X)

Numbers are chances in %; a full square always plays. Snapshot Y is busier, with more hats and perc and a few jumps in the matrix (step 4 sometimes repeats, step 8 sometimes jumps back). Governor moves towards Y as Long Form's energy rises.

Settings in the patch

The ChangesHouse, KEY F, MODE Style (minor), RATE 1 bar, LOOP 4, FOCUS 1, COLOUR noon, SPREAD 30%, DRIFT 20%. TENSION opens the pad filter a little.
WeightedPULL +50%, STEP 50%, RANGE 2, OCTAVE +1. Triggered by Markovian DIV (8ths), accent from Long Form BAR.
Long FormThe default form (Intro 16, Groove 32, Build 8, Peak 32, Break 16, Drop 8, Peak 2 32, Outro 16), DRIFT 15%.
DrumsInterlock grid as on the previous page, AMOUNT 70%, MEMORY 1. Rewrite: A and D locked. Governor: WINDOW 32, variety 30%, SAME → REWRITE NOW, VARIED → HOLD.

Playing it

  1. Let the Intro and the start of the Groove play. When you like the groove, press Rewrite's SEED. From then on the hats, perc and bass rhythm grow slowly, but only when Governor hears them repeating.
  2. Hold Rewrite's REWIND for a second at the Drop, so the drop lands on the bare seed.
  3. Press NEW on The Changes for a fresh chord loop in the current section, or LOCK to keep the one you have.
  4. Turn Weighted's PULL left in the Break for a floating melody, and back right for the Drop.
  5. Edit the grid in Long Form to change who plays where. The drums, bass, pad and lead all follow it.

Recipe 2Sunday gospel

A slow 4/4 in Ab at about 84 BPM, with rich chords that move every bar and a drummer who fills at the end of each section.

The ChangesSTYLE Gospel, KEY Ab, COLOUR three o'clock (11ths and 13ths), SPREAD noon, LOOP 8, RATE 1, FOCUS 1.5. Right-click → Voices 6.
WeightedPULL +75%, STEP 70%, right-click → Chord on V/Oct → "add the 3rd".
Long FormEnergy low in the verses, high at the Peak. Long Form FILL → Markovian EVOLVE CV, instead of Governor.
InterlockD → E +60 (a ghost note after the backbeat), A → B +40 (the bass follows the kick). MEMORY 1.
Rewrite, GovernorNot needed. Patch Interlock's outs straight to the voices.

In snapshot Y, give row 8 of the matrix a 40% jump to itself and E high chances on steps 6 to 8: those are the fills that FILL brings in. Listen for Bbm11 → Eb9 → Abmaj9 (ii, V, I) and the passing Ddim7 between IV and I. BASS sometimes takes the 3rd or 5th, as a gospel pianist's left hand would.

Recipe 3One-chord funk

E at about 100 BPM. One chord, a busy 16th groove, and a melody that only speaks in the gaps the drums leave.

The ChangesSTYLE Funk, KEY E, LOOP 2, RATE 2, COLOUR noon, FOCUS 2 (long vamps). Patch TOP → a clavinet-like voice with a short envelope triggered by track C.
WeightedPULL −40% for bluesy notes around the chord, RANGE 1. TRIG from Interlock's out F, not DIV.
MarkovianMatrix: straight, with a 20% jump to itself on steps 4 and 8 (stutters).
InterlockB → A −100 (no kick straight after a bass note), A → F −100 (the lead never lands on the kick), E → D +40 (ghost snare after the perc). MEMORY 1.
GovernorVariety TARGET 20%. VARIED → Markovian FREEZE gate: when it gets too busy the groove locks into a loop, and lets go once it's been repeating.

The loop sits on E9, sometimes E7#9, stepping to A9 and back. Turn PULL further left for more grit.

Recipe 4Detroit drift

C Dorian at about 128 BPM. Two-chord vamps and a drum part that grows on its own, with the chords and drums nudging each other.

The ChangesSTYLE Detroit, KEY C, LOOP 4, RATE 2, COLOUR three o'clock (m11), SPREAD full, DRIFT 30%. Patch TENSION → the pad filter's cutoff CV.
Long FormA long Groove (32 bars) and STEER low, so the form wanders between the calmer sections.
RewriteLOCK A and D. Track C: HIT BECOMES ●● (a roll), REST BECOMES ·●, EVERY 4, AMOUNT 40%. Track E: HIT BECOMES ·● (pushed late), EVERY 2. FIT: SQZ.
GovernorWINDOW 32, variety TARGET 30%. SAME → Rewrite REWRITE NOW, VARIED → Rewrite HOLD. Variety ERR → The Changes DRIFT CV.

Press SEED after a few bars. Governor waits until the hats and perc have been repeating, then lets Rewrite grow them. While the drums repeat, its variety ERR is positive and the chords drift more; once the drums change, the chords settle. Press The Changes' LOCK when it lands somewhere you love, and right-click → Restore this loop to go back.

Without Long Form

The Changes works on its own: clock 16ths into its CLOCK jack, and it changes chord every RATE bars with a single free-running loop. With no clock at all, each trigger at CHANGE moves to the next chord, handy for playing chords by hand from a button or a sequencer. The drum chain runs from the same clock. Without Long Form's ACT, Markovian's letter mutes arrange the drums, and its ACT out goes into Interlock instead.

If the drums misbehave

What you seeWhy, and what to do
Drums don't follow Long Form's gridLong Form ACT must go into Interlock's ACT IN, and Interlock's and Rewrite's ACT outs on down the chain.
Rewrite never changes anythingIt passes triggers through until you press SEED. Also check that HOLD isn't on and that the track isn't locked.
The drums are always busy (or always sparse)Governor's density TARGET knob adds to Long Form's energy: turn it fully left. Check its POL switch is down (+), and that Y is the busier snapshot.
Hits fall off the gridEvery module in the chain needs the same clock. Interlock in particular rolls its ghost hits just after the clock.
