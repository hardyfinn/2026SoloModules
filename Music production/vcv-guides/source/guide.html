Haer · VCV Rack 2 modules · user guide

The Changes,
Weighted and
Long Form

Three modules that play a whole track together. Long Form runs the song's sections, The Changes plays chords the way a style would, and Weighted writes a melody that leans on those chords.

The Changes (28 HP) and Weighted 2.6 (20 HP). Panel renders, not Rack screenshots.
Long Form (52 HP).
For Weighted plugin 2.6.0 (Weighted + The Changes) and LongFormMixxa 2.1.0 (Long Form + Mixxa). November 2026.

How the three fit together

Each module does one job, and two cables tie them together.

CableFrom → toWhat it carries
LINKLong Form → The Changes (and Mixxa)The beat, section changes, energy, reset and the section number. The Changes changes chord on Long Form's bar lines and keeps a separate chord loop for each section.
HARMThe Changes → WeightedThe chord now playing, plus the key, mode and style. Weighted takes its key and profile from it, and its melody lands on the chord's notes more often.

1 · Installing

  1. Get two files: Weighted-2.6.0-mac-arm64.vcvplugin (Weighted and The Changes) and LongFormMixxa-2.1.0-mac-arm64.vcvplugin (Long Form and Mixxa).
  2. In Rack choose Help → Open user folder, then quit Rack.
  3. Put both files, unopened, in the plugins-mac-arm64 folder, replacing any older Weighted or LongFormMixxa files.
  4. Start Rack. Search the module browser for The Changes, Weighted and Long Form.

Long Form must be 2.1.0 or later. Older versions don't send the section number, so The Changes can't keep a separate loop for each section.

2 · Sample setup: a house track in F minor

This patch plays about six minutes of house in F minor. A pad plays the chords, a bass follows the chord roots, and a lead melody sits on the chord tones. Long Form brings parts in and out, and the chords get richer as the energy rises.

Modules you need

ModuleUse
A clock16th notes at about 124 BPM, with a reset output (for example Impromptu Clocked, or Live through CV Tools and the disting NT)
Long FormThe song form
The ChangesThe chords
WeightedThe lead melody
VCV RandomRandom CV for Weighted to quantize
Three voicesPad: polyphonic VCO → VCF → VCA with an ADSR. Bass: VCO → VCF → VCA with an ADSR. Lead: VCO → VCF → VCA with an ADSR
Mixxa (optional)Long Form's lanes mute and unmute the parts for you

Patching, step by step

1
Clock. Clock 16ths → Long Form CLOCK. Clock reset → Long Form RESET.
2
LINK. Long Form LINK → The Changes LINK. If you use Mixxa, add a second cable from the same LINK jack → Mixxa LINK. The Changes now ignores its own CLOCK jack and follows Long Form.
3
HARM. The Changes HARM → Weighted HARM. Weighted's display shows HARM in its top line, and amber lines under the bars of the chord's notes.
4
Pad. The Changes CHORD → pad VCO V/OCT (polyphonic, 5 voices for House). The Changes GATE → pad ADSR gate. The gate drops for 5 ms at each chord change, so the envelope restarts on every new chord.
5
Bass. The Changes BASS → bass VCO V/OCT. For the rhythm, send an off-beat 8th from your clock (or a drum sequencer) → bass ADSR gate.
6
Melody. VCV Random → Weighted CV. Clock 16ths → Weighted TRIG (or 8ths for a calmer line). Long Form BAR → Weighted ACCENT, so the first note of every bar lands on a strong note.
7
Lead. Weighted V/OCT → lead VCO V/OCT. Weighted TRIG out → lead ADSR gate.
8
Out. Into Mixxa: bass on channel 2, pad on 6, lead on 7 (these match Long Form's lane names), and drums on 1, 3, 4 and 5 if you have them. Mixxa OUT L/R → Audio. Without Mixxa, send the voices to any mixer.

Key, mode and style are set only on The Changes. Weighted follows them over HARM, so its ROOT, SCALE and PROFILE knobs have no effect while HARM is patched.

What you'll hear

  1. Press play. Long Form starts the Intro and The Changes plays its first loop, starting on Fm9. A typical House loop is Fm9 → Dbmaj9 → Eb7 → Fm9 (i, bVI, bVII, i), one chord per bar.
  2. Each section gets its own loop. When Long Form moves to the Groove, The Changes starts a new loop on the next bar line. The Intro's loop comes back when the form returns to it.
  3. Energy brings colour. In the Build and the Peaks, Long Form's energy is high, so the chords move up the stack (9ths, then 11ths) and lean towards tense chords. The Break thins them out again.
  4. The melody follows. Weighted picks chord notes more often, and the first note after each chord change lands on the new chord's root or 3rd. The light by Weighted's HARM jack flashes when that happens.
  5. Slow change. After each pass of a loop there's a 20% chance DRIFT rewrites one chord (never the first). An amber dot in the display marks it. Press LOCK when you like a loop, or right-click → Restore this loop to undo the drift.

Playing it live

  • Click a chord in The Changes' display to roll it again. Right-click it to choose from the style's list, which shows each chord's name and Roman numeral; rare ones are marked.
  • HOLD stays on the current chord (or patch a gate to the HOLD jack).
  • NEW rolls a fresh loop on the next bar line.
  • Turn Weighted's PULL left of noon in the Break. The melody then avoids the chord notes and floats over the harmony. Turn it back right for the Drop.
  • Patch Long Form ENERGY into Weighted MOD A and press MOD A's dest button until the display shows A>PULL. The melody then sits closer to the chords as the track builds.

Without Long Form

The Changes works on its own: clock 16ths into its CLOCK jack, and it changes chord every RATE bars with a single free-running loop. With no clock at all, each trigger at CHANGE moves to the next chord, which is handy for playing chords by hand from a button or a sequencer.

More recipes. Four complete setups (house, gospel, funk and Detroit), with drums from the Francois Markovian plugin and a ready-made Rack patch of the house track, are in the separate Recipes document.

3 · The Changes

The Changes picks the next chord the way a player of the chosen style would. Each style has a table of likely moves (gospel's ii → V → I, house's i → bVI → bVII) and of the extensions it uses. Each new chord takes the inversion that moves the voices least.

The display shows the chord now playing, its Roman numeral and key, the style, the next chord, and the loop as a row of boxes. The orange box is playing now, and an amber dot means DRIFT rewrote that chord. LINK appears top right when Long Form is connected, and SECTION n shows which section's loop is playing.

ControlWhat it does
STYLEFlat, Fit, Folk, Classical, Downbeat, Rock, Detroit, House, Gospel, Funk, Soul, Reggae, Pathet 1–3. The same list as Weighted's profiles. Fit and Downbeat use the Classical chords.
KEYAround the circle of fifths, like Weighted's Root. Default F.
MODEStyle (each style's home mode: House minor, Detroit Dorian, Funk Mixolydian, the rest major), or major, minor, Dorian, Mixolydian.
RATEBars per chord when clocked: ½, 1, 2, 4, 8.
LOOPOff (a new chord every time), 2, 4 or 8 chords. Loops always start on the home chord.
FOCUS + CV0 = any chord in the key equally likely. 1 = the style as written. 3 = only its favourite moves. Rare chords from outside the style appear only at low Focus.
COLOUR + CVHow far up the chord goes: triad, 7th (from about 9 o'clock), 9th (noon), 11th, 13th (full). Each style only uses the extensions it would, so a reggae I stays a triad and a rock chord is a power chord at minimum Colour.
SPREAD + CVClose voicing, then drop-2 (from about 10 o'clock), then wide (from about 2 o'clock).
DRIFT + CVChance, after each pass of the loop, that one chord (never the first) is rewritten.
HOLDStay on this chord. The button latches; the HOLD jack holds while its gate is high. A CHANGE trigger still moves on.
NEWA fresh loop on the next bar line. With no clock, at once. Blinks while waiting.
LOCKNo drift edits.
TENSION lightsHow far the chord is from home, as on the TENSION output.

CV inputs: 10 V sweeps the knob's whole range and adds to the knob.

4 · Weighted: the HARM input and PULL

Weighted is a quantizer that picks notes by weight. Random CV goes in, and notes come out in your key, with strong notes chosen more often. From 2.5.0 it can also lean on a chord.

HARM and PULL sit in the grey block at the end of the length/strong row.

ControlWhat it does
HARM inA chord, polyphonic 1 V/oct, with channel 1 as the root (up to 6 notes). Any poly chord works; The Changes also sends key, mode and style.
PULLHow much the chord counts. Default +50%. Right of noon: the chord's notes come up more often, up to 4 times as often at full. Noon: no effect. Left of noon: the chord's notes become rarer (a quarter as often at full), so the melody takes the notes between them.
LightDim while HARM is patched. Bright when a note lands on a new chord's root or 3rd.

What changes when HARM is patched

  1. Key and style follow The Changes. Root, scale and profile come from the cable, and the display shows HARM in its top line. Right-click → HARM input → Keep my own knobs if you want Weighted's own settings. A plain poly chord from another module never changes the key.
  2. Chord notes get heavier, by PULL. The display draws an amber line under each chord note's bar, so you can watch the weights move with the harmony.
  3. Chord changes land. With PULL above noon, the first note after a chord change is drawn towards the new chord's root or 3rd.
  4. The Chord menu stacks chord notes. With right-click → Chord on V/Oct on, "add the 3rd" and "add the 5th" add the next notes of the current chord instead of scale thirds.
  5. PULL is a Mod destination. Press MOD A's or MOD B's dest button until the display shows PULL. 10 V sweeps PULL across its whole range.

The HARM cable, channel by channel

ChannelCarries
1–6Chord notes, 1 V/oct. Channel 1 is the root; unused channels repeat it
7How many chord notes there are, 1 V each
8Key root, 1/12 V per semitone above C
9Mode: 0 V major, 1 V minor, 2 V Dorian, 3 V Mixolydian
10Style: 0.5 V per profile, in Weighted's Profile order
11Chord change trigger

Weighted reads key, mode and style only when the cable has 10 or more channels, so a plain chord cable only sets the chord.

5 · Long Form: what matters for this setup

Long Form is an arrangement conductor. You draw a song form on an 8 × 8 grid, with sections down and lanes across, and it tells everything else when to come in, drop out and build. The full Long Form guide is in its README. These are the parts that matter here.

ControlWhat it does here
GridWhich lanes play in each section. Lanes 2 (bass), 6 (chords) and 7 (lead) are the voices in the sample setup. Mixxa mutes and unmutes them for you.
LENSection length in bars. The Changes keeps the same loop for the whole section.
ENERGY0 to 10 per section. Over LINK it adds COLOUR and tension in The Changes (right-click The Changes → Long Form energy drives: colour, tension, both or nothing).
STEER / FOLLOWLet Long Form choose the next section by energy. Each section still brings back its own chord loop.
DRIFTLong Form's own drift edits the form. The Changes' DRIFT edits the chords. They are independent.
BAR outA trigger each bar. In the sample setup it feeds Weighted's ACCENT.

LINK, channel by channel

ChannelCarriesUsed by
1–8Lane gates (10 V = play)Mixxa
9Beat triggerThe Changes (its clock), Mixxa
10Section change triggerThe Changes (switch loops), Mixxa
11Energy, 0–10 VThe Changes
12Reset triggerThe Changes, Mixxa
1316th triggerMixxa ("next 16th" timing)

6 · If something isn't working

What you seeWhy, and what to do
The Changes never changes chordNo clock is reaching it. Patch LINK from Long Form (and make sure Long Form is running), or a 16th clock into CLOCK. HOLD may also be on: its light shows.
Chords change in the wrong placeThe clock rate doesn't match. Right-click → Clock input and pick what your clock sends (16ths, 24 PPQN, 8ths or quarters). With LINK patched this doesn't matter.
All sections play the same loopLong Form is older than 2.1.0 and doesn't send the section number. Install LongFormMixxa 2.1.0.
Weighted's ROOT and SCALE knobs do nothingHARM from The Changes is setting the key. Change KEY and MODE on The Changes instead, or right-click Weighted → HARM input → Keep my own knobs.
The melody ignores the chordsPULL is at noon or below. Turn it right. Weighted's FOCUS at 0 also flattens every weight.
The pad plays too few notesThe pad VCO must be polyphonic (set its polyphony from the cable or its menu). Voices are set by style unless you choose a number in The Changes' right-click menu.
Chords sound too busyTurn COLOUR down, choose fewer voices, or turn SPREAD up so the notes have room.
The module doesn't appear in RackThe plugin file must sit unopened in plugins-mac-arm64, and Rack must be restarted. All of these plugins are numbered 2.x, which Rack 2 needs.
