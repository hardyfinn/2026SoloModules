Bigger labels on the other 18 panels

Every panel below uses the label sizes you approved for Weighted. Where the labels didn't fit, the panel got wider instead of more crowded. The jacks, knobs and buttons are the same ones as now; only their places move. Each module also has a suggestion for cutting down the number of jacks, like the Mod A/B change on Weighted.

3.2 mmknob, button and section labels (they were 1.6 to 2.3 mm)
2.9 mmjack labels and small-control labels
2.0 to 2.6 mmrange ends and short hints; longer hints moved into the tooltips

Wider panels push neighbouring modules along when an old patch opens, but every patch still loads with its cables in place. Merging jacks is different: a cable plugged into a jack that goes away would be lost, which is why those are only suggestions for now.
