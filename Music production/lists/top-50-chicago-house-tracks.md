# Top 50 Chicago house tracks (up to 2010)

Compiled 16 October 2026 from 10 published lists (sources below). Nothing released after 2010 is included. Tracks are ranked by how many lists they appear on, shown as **(n)**. Within a tie, order is editorial. For general house lists (Time Out, DJ Mag, Auralcrave), only the Chicago records were counted. Places 27 to 50 each appear on one list and were picked to cover the main styles.

## Caveats

- Mr. Fingers and Fingers Inc. versions of Can You Feel It were counted as one track.
- Years come from the source lists where given, otherwise from common release dates. Some lists disagree, so check before quoting.
- Detroit records on the general lists (such as Strings of Life and Good Life) and New York and UK records were left out.
- Tears was released in 1989 under Frankie Knuckles' name with New York's Satoshi Tomiie. It is kept because both lists that include it treat it as a Knuckles record.
