## Caveats

- Several lists lean heavily towards Detroit, which helps Detroit tracks at the top.
- Some pages showed only part of their list (Thump, Underground Talent), so a few counts may be low.
- Years are from the source lists or common release dates; check them before quoting.
- Non-techno crossover entries on the general lists (for example Avicii and Martin Garrix) were left out.
