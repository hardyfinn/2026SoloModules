# Top 50 UK techno tracks

Compiled 16 October 2026. There are only two dedicated UK techno lists online (Fact's 20 and Red Bull's 10). I combined them with the UK entries from nine general techno lists, plus two label and artist retrospectives. Tracks on two or more sources are ranked first, shown as **(n)**. The rest each appear on one source and are grouped by era. Some Fact entries are EPs or albums rather than single tracks.

## Caveats

- The evidence is thin. Only eight tracks appear on more than one source, and none on more than two, so this is closer to a curated guide than a vote.
- Places 9 to 50 are grouped by era rather than ranked.
- The British Murder Boys and Sandwell District counts combine a named track with the release it appears on.
- Years are from the source lists or common release dates; check them before quoting.
