# Mannequins Just Friends overview

## Summary
Just Friends is six linked slope generators sharing one set of controls.
Intone spreads them into harmonics or subharmonics; Range, Mode and the Run jack give envelopes, oscillators and six hidden modes.

## Question
> New thread. Mannequins just friends.

## Answer
**Just Friends in short:** it's six linked slope generators that share one set of controls. The **Time** knob and its 1V/oct input set the base rate. **Intone** spreads the six channels: at noon they all run together, turning it clockwise makes each one run as a harmonic (2×, 3×…6×), and turning it anticlockwise makes them subharmonics. **Ramp** sets the rise/fall balance and **Curve** bends the shape from log through linear to exp and sine.
- **Range:** *shape* gives envelopes and LFOs, *sound* gives audio-rate oscillators.
- **Mode:** *transient* fires once per trigger, *sustain* holds while the gate is high (an ASR), *cycle* loops it.
- **Outputs:** Identity plus 2N to 6N, and a Mix. A trigger into Identity is normalled down through all six channels.
- **Run jack:** a CV here turns on six hidden modes, one for each range and mode pairing: Shift, Strata, Volley (shape) and Spill, Plume, Floom (sound).

**How it fits your rig:** Metropolix or Hapax pitch into Time, plus six gates from Marbles, Pam's or DROID logic, in sound+sustain gives a six-voice harmonic chord from one note. In shape+cycle with Intone turned up, you get six related LFOs that drift in and out of phase, which is very Autechre for steering a patch across a whole arrangement.
