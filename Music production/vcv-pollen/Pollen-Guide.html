Pollen

A VCV Rack granular sampler for learning your hardware granular module while you're away from the case. It covers the same ideas, laid out clearly, and the display shows you what the grains are doing.

1. Install

  1. Download Pollen-2.0.0-mac-arm64.vcvplugin from the Releases page of the Grain repo, under "Latest build", or from this project's files.
  2. Put it in ~/Library/Application Support/Rack2/plugins-mac-arm64/ and restart Rack. Pollen appears under the "Haer" brand.

2. First sound in one minute

  1. Right-click Pollen and choose Load WAV.... Any WAV from any folder works, up to 32 seconds (longer files are trimmed).
  2. Patch OUT L / OUT R to Audio.
  3. Press LATCH. You hear grains, and the display shows them as blips inside the yellow window.
  4. Turn RATE and SIZE while you watch the blips. Fast and short gives a buzz, and slow and long gives overlapping smears.

3. The panel, section by section

SOUND

1 2 3 4Select a Sound and play it. With LATCH off, a Sound plays only while you hold its button (or while GATE is high). Bright means selected, dim means it has audio.
LATCHKeeps the selected Sound playing without holding anything.
SAMPLEPress to record the inputs into the selected Sound, and press again to stop (32 s max). On a Live Sound, SAMPLE freezes and unfreezes the live buffer instead.
THRULets you hear the input, which also goes through BLUR.
SCENE A / BChooses which Scene the knobs edit. Each Sound has two Scenes, each a complete set of knob settings and modulation depths.
MORPHBlends between Scene A (bottom) and Scene B (top). Most settings glide. SHAPE and REVERSE can't glide, so each grain picks A's or B's at random, weighted by the fader position.

WHERE IN THE AUDIO

STARTWhere the grain window begins (the yellow line).
WRAPHow wide the window is (the yellow shading). At 0, every grain starts at exactly the same spot.
SCANMoves the read point through the window. Noon is still, right moves forward, and left moves backward, up to 2x real time. The faint yellow line shows where it is.
SHAPEThe grain envelope, one of 7: bell, sinc, triangle, decay, ramp, tukey, square. Decay is percussive, ramp swells, and square sounds chopped.
REVERSEPlays grains backwards, set per Scene. Ping-pong (alternating directions) is in the right-click menu.
LEVELGrain volume. When lots of grains overlap, Pollen turns each one down so the mix stays usable.

GRAIN

RATEGrains per second, from 0.25 to 130 Hz. Fully left means one grain per trigger (a SOUND press, or a GATE rise). With a clock in SYNC, RATE steps through /16 /8 /4 /2 x1 x2 x4 x8 x16 of the clock.
SIZEGrain length, from 20 ms to 4 s.
PITCHGrain playback speed, ±2 octaves (±3 with modulation). The readout shows the hardware's PITCH LED colour: green on octaves, red in between, purple when quantized.
LINK RATE–SIZERATE now sets how many grains overlap (1x to 8x) instead of grains per second, so changing SIZE keeps the texture equally dense. With SYNC, SIZE also snaps to clock divisions.
LINK SIZE–PITCHPitching up shortens the grain, so each grain always covers the same slice of audio. Without it, grains stay the same length and pitching up reads more audio.
TONEPer-grain filter. Left is low-pass, right is high-pass, and noon is flat.
BLURReverb send. More BLUR also gives a longer tail.
MORPH CV amountAttenuverter for the MORPH CV input.

MOD DEPTH (X, Y, Z, RAND)

Press X. Its light blinks and the knobs now set how much X moves each parameter. Noon is none, right is more, and left is inverted. Press X again to go back to normal. RAND gives every grain a new random value, which suits START, PITCH, SIZE and SHAPE. If you set a negative RAND depth on LEVEL, grains get spread across the stereo field. Depths are saved per Scene, so MORPH blends the modulation too.

On the hardware you do the same thing by holding X and turning a knob.

4. Reading the display

  • Top strip: the selected Sound's audio. The yellow line is START, the shading is the WRAP window, the faint line is the SCAN position, and each blip is a grain, rising and falling with its envelope. A red line means recording.
  • Envelope box: Scene A's shape in blue, Scene B's in orange when it differs, and a dot following the newest grain. It also shows grain length and direction.
  • Morph box: the fader position, then one column per knob. The blue tick is the Scene A setting, the orange tick is Scene B, and the yellow bar is what you hear right now (morph plus CV). Watch the bars move as you push MORPH.
  • Readout: rate, pitch with the LED colour, tone, blur, level, scan, links and play state, all in words. In a MOD DEPTH mode it shows that source's depths as bars instead.

5. Lessons (each one carries straight over to the hardware)

  1. Freeze a moment. Set WRAP to 0 and SCAN to noon, with RATE around 20 Hz and SIZE around 150 ms. Sweep START slowly and you're "scrubbing" a frozen sound. Then try SHAPE square against bell.
  2. Time-stretch. Set WRAP to 100% and push SCAN slightly right of noon, with RATE–SIZE linked at 4x. The audio plays slowly at its original pitch. Now move PITCH and notice the timing doesn't change.
  3. Two Scenes, one gesture. On Scene A, make a tight percussive stutter (decay shape, small SIZE, high RATE). Press SCENE B and make a long dark smear (bell, big SIZE, TONE left, BLUR up). Sweep MORPH and watch the morph columns.
  4. Random but musical. Press RAND and turn PITCH a little right of noon, then press RAND again. Right-click and set Pitch quantizer to Minor pentatonic. The PITCH LED turns purple and the grains land on notes.
  5. One grain per step. Turn RATE fully left and patch a sequencer gate into GATE. Each step fires one grain. Add X depth on START from a stepped voltage to pick slices.
  6. Live granular. Right-click and turn on Live for Sound 4, then patch a source into IN L/R. Grains come from the last few seconds of input. Press SAMPLE to freeze the buffer and keep playing with what was captured.

6. In your rig

  • Clock: send a DROID clock (or a clock from Ableton through the disting NT) into SYNC. RATE becomes a divider, and the header shows the bpm. Patch a 1-bar pulse into NEXT, set to Reset SCAN in the menu, so the scan restarts on the downbeat.
  • Marbles: X outputs into X/Y/Z, with depth on START and PITCH, give drifting Autechre-style motion. Marbles' t-gates into GATE with RATE fully left give one grain per trigger.
  • Arrangement: patch a very slow DROID LFO or a Live automation lane into MORPH. One loop then becomes an 8–16 bar journey from Scene A to Scene B, a direct fix for loop fatigue.
  • SELECT: 0–5 V picks Sound 1–4. Sequence it from Metropolix or K-Accumulator to switch sounds per step.
  • Outputs A/B (menu): send the grain trigger to a drum module, or the grain envelope to a filter, so the rest of the patch follows the grains.

7. Differences from the hardware

  • 4 Sounds instead of 8, and no Presets or Projects. Sounds and settings are saved with the VCV patch.
  • Hidden combos and ALT pages are right-click menu items, and each item names the hardware combo. See On the hardware... at the bottom of the menu.
  • SAMPLE records straight into the selected Sound, so there's no separate save step. The always-listening buffer is under Grab the last seconds of input.
  • Pollen's reverb, filter, envelopes and knob ranges are close in spirit to the hardware but not exact copies, so expect the sound to differ in detail.
