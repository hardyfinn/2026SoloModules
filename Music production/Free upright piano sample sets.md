# Free upright piano sample sets

## Summary
Four free upright piano sample sets compared by velocity layers, sampling density and licence.
VS Upright No. 1 has 3 layers plus alternate takes; VSCO-2 and FreePats are CC0.

## Question
> Make 4 demos of the same short phrase from 4 different free to download upright piano sample sets. I would be interested in sample sets that have more than, well, let me know the complexity of the sample set. Medium complexity would be good rather than just individual keys at one velocity level.

## Answer
Here are four free uprights playing the same phrase. It's a pedalled ii-V-I and a soft run, then a louder answer so you can hear the top velocity layers. I matched the loudness so they're fair to compare.

1. FreePats Upright KW: a Kawai upright in a living room. It has **2 velocity layers** and a sample every 3 keys. Public domain (CC0).
2. KeyPleezer LivingRoom Upright, Micro edition: **2 crossfaded velocity layers, recorded with the pedal both up and down**, a sample every 2 to 3 keys. CC BY-SA, so you'd need to give credit and share anything made from the samples under the same licence. The full free edition has 6 layers but needs an email signup.
3. VSCO-2 Upright Piano (Ivy Audio): **3 velocity layers**, a sample every 4 keys. CC0.
4. VS Upright No. 1 (Versilian): **3 velocity layers plus 2 alternate takes per note** (so repeated notes vary), a sample every 6 keys. CC0.

All four are medium complexity, and none is a single-velocity set. Numbers 3 and 4 are the richest you can download freely without signing up. The CC0 ones could also be bundled in a VCV module and shared without licence worries. A fifth set I found (K18 Upright, 7 layers) has a dead download site.
