# Jack: 32 preset patterns (draft for review)

16 house and 16 techno classics, all released before 2000. Each is one bar of 16 steps, mapped onto the 909's 11 voices plus accent.
Only rows that play are shown. `x` = hit, `.` = rest. Step 1 is the downbeat.

**How these were made:** they are my own one-bar reconstructions of each record's drum part, written from knowledge of the records. They are not checked against published transcriptions.
Many of these records used a 707, 808, Linn or samples rather than a 909, so each is "that groove played on a 909". Mark any that feel wrong and I'll redo them.
