First patch

  1. Add Metroplex, a VCO, an envelope (ADSR) and a VCA.
  2. Patch PITCH to the VCO's V/Oct and GATE to the ADSR's gate. VCO into the VCA, ADSR into the VCA's CV, VCA to your audio out.
  3. Leave CLOCK empty. Metroplex runs on its own clock at 120 BPM, and each pulse is a 16th note.
  4. Move a few sliders. Set stage 4's PULSES to 3 and its GATE to yellow (multi): it plays three 16ths in a row. Set stage 8 to blue (hold) with 4 pulses for a long note.
To sync with Live, patch a 16th-note clock (CV Tools through the disting NT) into CLOCK and Live's transport reset into RESET.

Stage controls

Each of the 16 columns is one stage. From top to bottom:

ControlWhat it does
PITCH sliderThe stage's note. The slider covers the Slider range (1 to 4 octaves) and always lands on a note in the scale. Hover to see the note name. The handle lights up on the stage that is playing.
PULSES 1-8How many clock pulses the stage lasts.
GATEClick to step through the four gate types (see below).
SLIDEGlide into this stage's note. The Slide page sets how.
MUTLit = Mutate may change this stage. Off = locked, always plays as set.
SKIP The stage is left out: the sequence jumps straight past it. If every stage is skipped, it keeps time in silence.

PULSES (all stages)

The small knob at the top right sets every stage's pulse count at once. Turning it overwrites all 16 PULSES knobs, and you can then change them one by one again.

Display and menus

Six page buttons pick what the display shows: HOME, CLOCK, SEQ, SCALE, SLIDE and MUTATE. Each CTRL knob also has its own menu button.

  • Change the highlighted row: drag the DATA encoder up or down, or scroll over it. Hold Shift for steps of 10.
  • Next row: click the encoder, or press the page button again. You can also click a row on the display.
  • On HOME, turning the encoder flips through the pages.
  • Right-click the display for a menu of everything on that page, with named lists and sliders.
A cyan C1-C4 badge on a row means that CTRL knob is setting it right now. The row shows the knob's value, and you can't change it from the menu until you unassign the knob.

Sequence: start, length and order

Scale

Every note is pulled to the nearest note of the chosen Scale and Root, so the sliders can't play a wrong note. Slider range sets how many octaves a slider covers: 1 octave gives fine control, 4 gives big leaps. Octave shifts everything up or down.

The TRANSP jack adds 1 V/oct before the scale, so transposing keeps the notes in key.

Slides

Turn on SLIDE on a stage, then pick a type on the SLIDE page:

TypeHow it glides
TempoThe glide takes the same time whatever the interval. Slide amount is that time as a share of one pulse (100% = a full pulse).
AnalogA fixed speed, so big jumps take longer, like a slew limiter. 100% = 2 seconds per octave, 25% = half a second.
Acid303 style. A slide stage glides out of itself into the next note. Its gate is held and tied into the next note with no gap, so the envelope doesn't retrigger. This works even if the stage's gate is set to rest.

Mutate

Mutate varies your pattern without losing it. The MUTATE knob runs from your pattern as programmed to fully mutated:

0: as programmed
a few stages change
more change
full: everything allowed changes

As you turn it up, more stages change: a note moves by a few scale degrees, a pulse count or gate type changes, and if you allow it, slides and skips flip. Turn it back and the changes undo in reverse order, so 0 is always your original.

The seed

The changes come from a seed, a number shown on the MUTATE page. The same seed and knob position always give the same result, so a version you like is repeatable. Press SEED (yellow) or send a trigger to the SEED jack for a fresh set of variations of the same pattern.

What can change

MUTATE page rowEffect
PitchOff, or up to ±12 scale degrees (default ±3)
RhythmPulse counts and gate types (on by default)
SlidesSlides flip on or off (off by default)
SkipsSkips flip, from about a third of the way up (off by default)
Turn MUT off on the stages that hold your riff together, like the downbeats. Mutate will vary the rest around them.

Snapshots

8 snapshots, each holding the 16 stages (pitch, pulses, gate, slide, MUT, skip), Start stage, Length, Play order and the Mutate seed. Scale, tempo and the CTRL knobs stay the same across snapshots.

  1. Save: press SAVE (red, it starts blinking), then a number button. Press SAVE again to cancel. A saved snapshot's number glows dimly.
  2. Recall: press a number. The current snapshot is fully lit. One that is waiting to come in blinks.

When it switches

Snapshot switch on the SEQ page:

  • End of cycle (default): waits for the current pass to finish, so changes land on the bar.
  • Now: switches straight away.

CTRL knobs

Each of the four CTRL knobs can be pointed at a setting. Choose it with the knob's menu button and the DATA encoder, or right-click the knob. Each one has a CV jack (CTRL 1-4 on the bottom row) and a CV amt attenuverter beneath it:

full left: CV upside down
noon: CV does nothing
full right: ±5 V sweeps the whole range

New modules start with CTRL 1 on Gate length, CTRL 2 on Slide amount, and CTRL 3 and 4 unassigned.

KindHow it actsDestinations
DirectThe knob is the settingTempo, Clock div, Gate length, Start stage, Length, Play order, Scale, Slider range, Slide amount, Mutate pitch, Mutate rhythm
OffsetNoon = no change, left subtracts, right addsPitch pre (±12 semitones, then back into the scale), Pitch post (±12 semitones, plain transpose), Octave (±2), Pulse count (±7 on every stage)

If two knobs set the same direct setting, the higher-numbered CTRL wins. Two offsets on the same destination add together.

Ideas to try

1. Evolving line

Program a riff and turn MUT off on stages 1, 5, 9 and 13. Set CTRL 1 to Mutate amount and patch a slow LFO into its CV. The line drifts away and comes back. Press SEED for new variations, and SAVE one you like.

2. Arrangement from snapshots

Save an intro, a verse and a breakdown into snapshots 1-3. Leave Snapshot switch on End of cycle and press the numbers live, or automate the SNAP jack from Live through CV Tools to play the arrangement back.

3. Moving window

CTRL 1 on Length, CTRL 2 on Pulse count, CTRL 3 on Start stage. Start short for the intro, open to 16, then add pulses for a stretched breakdown.

4. Acid bass

Slide type Acid, amount 30%. Gates mostly Single, slides on two or three stages, and one or two SKIPs to shorten the bar. Put Pitch pre on CTRL 2 to move the riff through the scale.

5. Converging chords

Play order Converge on 8 stages into Just Friends or Three Sisters for an outside-in call and response.

Installing (Apple Silicon Mac)

  1. In Rack, choose Help → Open user folder, then quit Rack.
  2. Put Metroplex-2.1.0-mac-arm64.vcvplugin, unopened, into the plugins-mac-arm64 folder. Delete any older Metroplex file there.
  3. Start Rack and search for Metroplex.
Patches saved with 2.0 open with most MUT buttons off, and the old accumulator settings are gone.
