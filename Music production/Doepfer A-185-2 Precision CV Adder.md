# Doepfer A-185-2 Precision CV Adder

## Summary
There is no Doepfer A-158-2; the module meant is almost certainly the A-185-2 Precision CV Adder.
It adds or subtracts up to four control voltages accurately, so it works as a pitch CV transposer and mixer.

## Question
> What does doepfer  a158-2 do?

## Answer
Doepfer doesn't make an A-158-2. There's no A-158 of any kind in their range. The closest match is almost certainly the **A-185-2 Precision CV Adder**, with the digits swapped. Funnily, the doepfer.de address for "a1582" opens the A-185-2 page.

**What it does:** it adds or subtracts up to four control voltages very accurately (resistors matched to 0.1%), so pitch CVs stay in tune when you combine them. In practice it's a manual transposer and mixer for pitch CV. It's 6 HP and costs about €100.

**Controls and jacks**
• Input 1 has a level knob, for modulation like an LFO or envelope. With nothing plugged in, that knob becomes a fine-tune control.
• Inputs 2 to 4 have no knob, so they're meant for 1V/oct pitch sources.
• Each input has a 3-way switch: add, off or subtract. With nothing plugged into that input, the switch adds or subtracts 1 V, which is one octave.
• Outputs: three normal and one inverted.

**Using it in your rig**
1. **Hands-on key changes:** Metropolix pitch goes into input 2, and the output goes to Three Sisters or Mangrove. Flipping the empty-input switches moves the line up or down by octaves live. That's a simple, physical way to give a section somewhere to go, which helps with loop fatigue.
2. **Drifting melodies, Autechre style:** add Marbles X (slow, smooth) to the Metropolix pitch, then quantize in a disting NT quantizer algorithm. The riff keeps its shape but wanders between notes. Send the inverted output to Just Friends for a mirrored counter-line.

To be honest, DROID and the disting NT can already do all of this in software, so you don't need the module. Its appeal is having physical switches to perform with.

If you meant a different Doepfer, the **A-155-2** (8-step analog sequencer) or the **A-156** (dual quantizer) are the next most likely. Tell me and I'll cover that one instead.
