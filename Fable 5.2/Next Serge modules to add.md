# Next Serge modules to add

## Summary
The recommended next six Serge modules are TKB, SEQ-PRG, Wilson Analog Delay, Frequency Shifter, BDR and Pulse Divider with Boolean logic.
The TKB touch keyboard sequencer is the biggest gap, since the set had no sequencer or keyboard of its own.

## Question
> What would be the next round of serge modules to add? these are excellent.

## Answer
Glad they're working for you. I'd add these six next, most important first:

1. **TKB, Touch Keyboard Sequencer.** The biggest gap: the set has no sequencer or keyboard of its own yet.
2. **SEQ-PRG, Sequencer Programmer.** Serge's smaller 8-stage sequencer, with four rows and voltage-controlled stage selection. It's the classic partner for the ASR and DSG.
3. **Wilson Analog Delay.** Bucket-brigade delay for chorus, flanging and Karplus-Strong plucks, which pair well with the pinged filter.
4. **Frequency Shifter.** Serge's inharmonic metal and barber-pole sounds. Nothing in the set does this yet.
5. **BDR, Bi-Directional Router.** A voltage-controlled switch that makes patches move between routes. It's a staple of complex Serge systems.
6. **Pulse Divider and Boolean logic.** Adds proper rhythm maths to COM and STR.

After that, the Phaser, the Extended ADSR and the Quad VCA/Gator would be good additions. TKB and the Wilson Delay are the two you left out of round one, so tell me if you'd rather skip them again. I can write the spec and build them the same way as round one.
